Workflow

Three steps from source to finished clips.

01

Ingest

Paste a video, playlist, or channel URL from YouTube, Twitch, or Kick, upload a local file, or auto-watch a channel for live streams and new uploads.

02

Transcribe & Score

Audio is transcribed locally with word-level timestamps. Your AI model scores moments using speech, audio loudness, chat spikes, and YouTube retention peaks.

03

Render & Publish

Clips are cut with burned-in ASS captions, hooks, and your chosen framing layout, then queued or scheduled to YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.

Capabilities

Built for channels, streamers, and editors.

YouTube channel & playlist support

Batch-queue playlists or channel uploads, poll channel RSS feeds automatically, and use YouTube Most-Replayed heatmaps and chapters for better picks.

Five framing layouts

Choose blurred fill, zoomed fill, centre crop, automatic corner facecam detection (gameplay), or top/bottom speaker split for podcasts and interviews.

Rolling-buffer live clipping

Record live streams in 6-second segments, monitor loudness and chat activity, and export finished clips before the stream even ends.

Scheduled multi-platform posting

Connect YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok via OAuth with per-creator minimum scores, spacing, daily caps, native YouTube scheduling, and view tracking.

FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before running your first video.

Is ClipForge free?

Yes. ClipForge is open source under the MIT License. You run it on your own computer or server with no subscription fees.

Which AI models are supported?

Bring an API key for Gemini, OpenAI, Grok, Groq, Claude, or OpenRouter, or use a local Ollama model. Without a key, ClipForge falls back to audio loudness picking.

What layouts and aspect ratios are supported?

Vertical 9:16 (blur, zoom, crop, gameplay facecam, and 2-speaker split screen) for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok, plus optional standard YouTube uploads without #Shorts.

Can I trim or restyle clips after generation?

Yes. Open any clip in the editor to adjust start/end timestamps, edit the hook, title, or captions, switch layout or caption style, and re-render.
